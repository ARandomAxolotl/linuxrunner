== ghostty-web (rcarmo/ghostty-web 0.9.3; ghostty-web.js, ghostty-vt.wasm)
Ghostty-Web Terminal Renderer
==============================

Source:   https://github.com/rcarmo/ghostty-web
Commit:   4e8a47ee4a60 (latest as of 2026-06-02)
Version:  0.9.3
Files:    ghostty-web.js, ghostty-vt.wasm

Patches applied for WKWebView/iOS embedding:
- Exported as `window.ghosttyWeb` (no ES module import in WKWebView)
- WASM loaded from embedded data: URL (no fetch in file:// context)
- Canvas2D fallback renderer (WebGL context lost recovery)
- iPhone backing store scale capped to 3x (prevents OOM on Pro Max)
